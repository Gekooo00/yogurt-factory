Why join us?

A proven concept

Since 2011, Yogurt Factory has turned a simple idea into an international network, with 23 company-owned stores: we apply to our own stores what we recommend to you.

🏆

The market leader

France's no.1 in frozen yogurt, in a steadily growing “lighter indulgence” segment.

Support

We train you, we stand by you

Our franchise team supports you before, during and after the opening.

  1. Before

    Preparing the project

    Help finding and approving the location, putting the financing together and planning the store layout.

  2. Training

    2 weeks to become a pro

    Hands-on training in store and theory: production, hygiene, management, HR, customer service.

  3. Opening

    5 days by your side

    A network coordinator is on site to launch your store in the best possible conditions.

  4. After

    Ongoing support

    Help with processes and management, new products, national and local marketing campaigns, central purchasing.

Who we're looking for

You're made for the Facto if…

  • You have an entrepreneurial spirit and a flair for retail
  • You enjoy managing and motivating a team
  • Customer service comes naturally to you
  • You have a personal contribution of around €30,000
  • You get personally involved in running the store
  • You have an area or premises in mind (that's a plus!)

Let's talk about your project

Do I need restaurant experience?

No. The initial training covers everything you need to know. Retail or management experience is, however, a real plus.

Can I open abroad?

Yes: the brand is already in 10 countries, from Europe to Asia and Africa. Tell us about your project and we'll look at it carefully.

Can I open several stores?

It's possible and even encouraged. Let's discuss it at our first meeting.
