Halal & allergens

Good to know before you order

✅ Halal

Our ice creams (frozen yogurt and vanilla soft serve) and our bubble waffles are halal. The milk we use is pasteurised and sterilised.

Please note: the Tagada, Smurfs and Marshmallow sweets contain pork gelatine.

⚠️ Allergens

Our products may contain milk, gluten, eggs, soya, peanuts and tree nuts. The detailed allergen list for each product, topping and sauce is available in store: please ask before ordering.

Indicative menu: products and flavours may vary between stores. Prices are displayed in each store.
