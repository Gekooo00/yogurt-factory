The Facto is what you make it

Light frozen yogurt, a giant topping bar and just one rule: treat yourself. Since 2011, we've been filling pots… and putting smiles on faces.

The concept

One pot, one price, zero limits

It's simple: choose the size of your pot, we fill it with our 0% fat frozen yogurt or our vanilla soft serve, and off you go to the topping bar. Fresh fruit cut every day, sauces, biscuits, sweets, cereals… add whatever you like.

As long as it fits in the pot, the price stays the same. A formula unique in France that makes every pot your own creation.

  • A lighter alternative to traditional ice cream
  • The biggest topping bar in France: 34 toppings and 11 sauces
  • Halal ice creams and bubble waffles, made with pasteurised and sterilised milk
  • Fresh products, prepared right in front of you
  • Customisation at the heart of the experience

Our story

From the American dream to France's no.1

It all started with a trip to the United States. Fresh HEC graduates Ouriel Hodara and Emmanuel Tedesco discovered self-serve frozen yogurt there… and decided to give it a French twist.

  1. 2011

    The first pot

    Aged 23, the two founders open the very first Yogurt Factory store.

  2. 2016

    Franchise launch

    Building on the success of its own stores, the network opens up to entrepreneurs.

  3. 2017

    Record growth

    Yogurt Factory enters the Financial Times ranking of Europe's 1,000 fastest-growing companies (2017 and 2018).

  4. 2018

    Boon acquisition

    The Facto takes over rival brand Boon and its stores.

  5. 2019

    Going international

    More than 40 stores, and the first openings outside France.

  6. 2024

    Ever more treats

    Bubble waffles, Liège waffles, bubble tea: the menu grows beyond the pot.

  7. 2025

    90 stores

    Donuts, vanilla soft serve, matcha, ube and slushies arrive. The network has 90 locations in 10 countries, from France to Uzbekistan.

  8. 2026

    Target: 100

    Launch of the loyalty programme (€1 = 1 point) and major new openings. What if the next one were yours?
