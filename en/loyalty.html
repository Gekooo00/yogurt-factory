Loyalty FAQ

Your questions

Do I need to download an app?

No. Your phone number is all it takes: we enter it at the till or you type it on the ordering kiosk.

How are points calculated?

€1 spent = 1 point, on the total amount of your order.

Can I use my points in every store?

The programme is available in participating stores. Ask the team at your favourite Facto.

How do I use my points?

When you order, at the till or on the kiosk, choose the reward you want: the matching points are deducted from your balance.
