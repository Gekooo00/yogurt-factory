What we use

This website uses no advertising, social media or analytics cookies. That's why you aren't shown a consent banner (article 82 of the French Data Protection Act and CNIL guidelines).

Only strictly necessary storage, exempt from consent, is used in your browser:

NamePurposeTypeDuration
yf-a11yRemember your accessibility settings (text size, contrast…)Browser local storage, only if you change a settingUntil you reset or clear it
yf-fav-store, yf-tb, yf-lang-hintRemember your favourite store and whether you closed the top banner or the language suggestionBrowser local storage, only if you use these featuresUntil you clear it

Third-party services

The store map loads images from OpenStreetMap, which sets no cookies. Buttons to TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Google Maps and delivery platforms are simple links: no content from these services is loaded unless you click.

Managing trackers

You can clear the data stored by this website at any time from your browser settings, or with the “Reset” button in the accessibility menu.
