What is frozen yogurt?

All the indulgence, none of the fat

Our ice cream is made from yogurt and served soft, nice and creamy. The result: an ultra-smooth texture, a slightly tangy note that keeps you coming back… and 0% fat in the ice cream. More of a vanilla fan? Our vanilla soft serve is waiting for you.

  • Frozen yogurt with 0% fat
  • Fresh fruit prepared every day, right in front of you
  • Halal (ice creams and bubble waffles), made with pasteurised and sterilised milk
  • A set price per pot size, toppings included
